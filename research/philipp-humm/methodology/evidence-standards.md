# Evidence standards — Philipp Humm research

## Tier-1 sources

- Articles and landing pages on **storylab.co** authored or attributed to Philipp Humm.
- Videos on the official YouTube channel **@Philipp-Humm**.

## Status labels (catalog + discovery log)

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **verified-framework** | Named framework with explicit steps/labels in ≥2 Tier-1 sources; listed in catalog `verified_frameworks` |
| **verified-teaching-point** | Recurring practice or idea explicit in ≥2 Tier-1 sources (may not be a named framework) |
| **single-source** | Explicit definition in one Tier-1 source only |
| **lead-unverified** | Name or topic appears; definition not yet cross-checked or incomplete |
| **unresolved-relationship** | Two sources compared; equivalence not demonstrated (see `paired_sources`) |
| **not-applicable** | Bio, marketing landing, or no teachable claim extracted |
| **interpretation** | Our paraphrase or inference — not attributed to Humm |
| **inaccessible** | Could not access page, transcript, or date — reason recorded |

## Extract rules

- Short, citation-sized verbatim excerpts only.
- Every excerpt: source ID, URL, publication date (when shown on page), optional video timestamp.
- Do not invent quotations, definitions, transcripts, or dates.
- Distinguish **quote** (block) from **note** (our wording, tagged interpretation).

## Verification gate for future `concepts/`

No concept files until a item reaches **verified-teaching-point** (or is documented as **single-source** with explicit scope).
