# SRC-014 — 5-P confidence loop (article)

## Excerpts (verbatim)

> I call it the 5-P confidence loop.

> Let's start with the first P, proof.

> Confidence isn't a personality trait, it's a memory bank of brave moments.

> But let's now go to the second P, practice.

> Real confidence doesn't come from knowing your script cold, it comes from being okay when things go off script

> Third P, prime.

> The fix is what I call priming.

> But let's now move to the next P, purpose.

> The shift is this: go in as a giver.

> But let's now move to the last P, praise.

> So, that's the confidence loop: Proof, Practice, Prime, Purpose, and Praise.

> The wins you find in praise, well, they become the proof you carry into your next talk.
