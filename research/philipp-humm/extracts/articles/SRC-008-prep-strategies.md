# SRC-008 — 4 strategies to prepare for a presentation

**URL:** https://storylab.co/prep-strategies/  
**Date on page:** March 28, 2023

## Excerpts (verbatim)

> In May 2022, I gave my first ever TEDx talk on the Secret to Building Lasting Confidence.

> ## Strategy #1: Visualize the worst

> ask yourself: "What would be the worst thing that could happen? Then go ahead and visualize that worst thing, but also you overcome it.

> ## Strategy #2: Speak in different voices … reading out the script in different voices, intensity, and accents.

> ## Strategy #3: Talk to a tree … pick a tree that was about 10 meters (or 30 feet ) away, and then I gave my talk to that tree (aka my audience).

> ## Strategy #4: Embarrass yourself before big moment … constructive embarrassment.
