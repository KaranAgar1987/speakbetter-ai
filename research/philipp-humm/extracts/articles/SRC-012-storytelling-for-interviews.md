# SRC-012 — Storytelling for interviews

**URL:** https://storylab.co/storytelling-for-interviews/  
**Date on page:** March 10, 2022

## Excerpts (verbatim)

> the simple Situation-Complication-Resolution (SCR) method works best.

> Situation: Describe the situation you were in prior to the challenge… Keep this part very concise…

> Complication: Describe the challenge… This is the most important part.

> Resolution: … focus on what you did.

> DON'Ts: … Rehearse in front of a mirror … Learn every single word by heart

> DOs: … Rehearse speaking out loud while walking outside. … Record yourself on video … Rehearse a minimum of 3 times before the interview.

> Your story should not be longer than 2-3 minutes.
