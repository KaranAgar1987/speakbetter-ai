# SRC-001 — Storytelling structure (CART)

**URL:** https://storylab.co/structure/  
**Date on page:** April 22, 2024  
**Evidence status (audit):** **verified framework** — see `sources/discovery-log.md` (SRC-001 + SRC-002).

## Excerpts (verbatim)

> I've developed a short, but powerful structure, called C.A.R.T. It consists of 1) Context, 2) Adversity, 3) Resolution, 4) Takeaway.

> **Step 1. Context** In the first step, set the scene by giving some background. Where and when is the story happening? Who's the main character, and what do they want?

> **Step 2. Adversity** Moving on, it's time to introduce the challenges the main character is facing.

> **Step 3. Resolution** Next, reveal how the character responds to the challenge and what happens in the end.

> **Step 4. Takeaway** In the final step, share what you learned from the experience. … Instead of saying "You should take away," frame it as "What I learned from that experience."

> Take any challenge that you've faced in your life and structure it according to the 4 steps: Context, Adversity, Resolution and Takeaway. Or CART.

## Note (interpretation)
