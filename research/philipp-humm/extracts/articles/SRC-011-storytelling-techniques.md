# SRC-011 — 5 storytelling techniques

## Excerpts (verbatim)

> 99.9% are NOT born that way. They train to become great storytellers.

> ## Storytelling Technique #1: Tell a personal story

> ## Storytelling Technique #2: Make the characters relatable

> ## Storytelling Technique #3: Use Emotions … Pause: When something surprising happens in your story, pause and feel the moment for a few seconds.

> ## Storytelling Technique #4: Use Dialogue … create the dialogue as you think it happened.

> ## Storytelling Technique #5: Cut Unnecessary Details … Most leadership stories fall into the range of 3-5min.
